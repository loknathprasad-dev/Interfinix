About Interfinix

AI‑powered interview preparation platform built for students and job seekers.

Our Vision

Interfinix helps students master technical interviews through personalised AI mock practice sessions. From campus placements to dream company interviews, the platform prepares users for real‑world challenges.

Every feature is designed with student needs in mind – from resume analysis to job‑specific question banks and progress tracking – so that interview anxiety can turn into confidence.

Interfinix is designed for students, freshers, and early‑career professionals preparing for internships, campus placements, and technical interviews in software and related fields.
The platform builds structured rounds based on your chosen role, difficulty level and resume, then generates domain‑specific questions that feel like a real interview.
Your voice and text responses are used only to improve your preparation experience and are not shared with recruiters or third parties without your consent.
Interfinix can start as a free tool for students, with optional premium features such as advanced analytics, detailed AI feedback, or company‑specific packs.
You only need a browser and stable internet; for best results, use a laptop with a microphone for voice‑based practice.
